# **App Name**: SibolMarket

## Core Features:

- Farmer Product Listings: Farmers and cooperatives can create, update, and manage product listings with details like price, quantity, availability, location, and harvest information.
- Buyer Browsing & Pooled Purchasing: Buyers (retailers, restaurants) can browse available product listings, view detailed information, and initiate individual or pooled purchase orders to meet minimum bulk requirements.
- Blockchain Escrow Payments: Securely lock buyer payments in a blockchain-based smart contract (on Base) which are released to farmers only upon confirmation of delivery.
- Logistics Status Updates: Logistics partners can update shipment statuses, allowing buyers to track their orders and providing basic transport cost estimates.
- Reputation & Review System: Users can rate and review farmers, buyers, and logistics providers after transactions to foster trust and accountability within the marketplace.
- Market Price Suggester Tool: An AI tool that provides suggested optimal pricing for farmer listings, leveraging available historical data or similar product offerings to enhance farmer income potential.
- Web3 Wallet Connection: Users can connect their compatible crypto wallets (e.g., MetaMask) to authenticate, manage their digital identity, and facilitate blockchain transactions.

## Style Guidelines:

- The application employs a light color scheme, conveying transparency and a natural, fresh aesthetic. The primary brand color, a sophisticated earthy green (#86A659), reflects the agricultural origins. The background color, a very light, almost off-white with a hint of green (#F3F4F0), ensures readability and a clean interface. The accent color, a vibrant golden yellow (#EDE15E), is used for calls-to-action and important highlights, drawing attention effectively.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) provides a modern, neutral, and highly legible appearance suitable for clear presentation of product details, prices, and marketplace information. Code font: 'Source Code Pro' (monospace) is recommended for any display of smart contract addresses or transaction hashes, maintaining clarity for technical details.
- Use clean, contemporary line-art icons throughout the platform. Icons should clearly represent marketplace actions (e.g., shopping cart, listings), agricultural elements (e.g., rice, farm produce), blockchain concepts (e.g., secure lock, wallet), and logistics (e.g., delivery truck).
- The layout is designed to be responsive and mobile-first, ensuring accessibility across various devices. Utilize a grid or card-based system for product listings and user profiles to promote visual clarity and easy browsing. Key information should be prominently displayed, with intuitive navigation paths.
- Incorporate subtle and tasteful animations for transitions between pages or states, loading indicators, and confirmation messages. These should enhance the user experience by providing feedback without causing distraction.
